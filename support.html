AI Photo Help

Support

Questions, bug reports or ideas: open an issue on GitHub. Please include your device, iOS or macOS version, and what you were doing.

Common questions

The AI critique is slow. On iPhone it takes about 40–50 seconds per photo with the default model (Qwen3-VL 2B). Keep the app open while it runs, because iOS pauses on-device AI in the background. The Smart scope (Settings) critiques only the contenders, which saves time.
Which iPhones can run the AI critique? iPhone 15 Pro or newer (8 GB of memory). Other iPhones still get focus, exposure, faces and eyes, burst grouping and ranking.
Will it change or delete my photos? No. Photos are only read. "Reject" is just a label inside the app.
How do I free the space used by the AI model? Settings ▸ AI model ▸ Delete model.
