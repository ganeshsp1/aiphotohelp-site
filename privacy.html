AI Photo Help

Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

AI Photo Help is built so that your photos stay on your device. This policy covers the iPhone app and the Mac app.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services. The developer receives no data about you or your photos.

Your photos

  • The iPhone app asks for access to your Photos library so it can analyse the photos you choose. The Mac app reads the folders or memory cards you pick.
  • All analysis runs on your device: focus, exposure, faces and eyes (using Apple's on-device Vision and Core Image frameworks), similar-frame grouping, and the AI critique.
  • Photos are only read. The app never edits, moves, deletes or uploads them. The only change it can make on iPhone is Add picks to album, which creates or updates an album when you tap it.
  • Analysis results, your decisions and settings are stored only inside the app on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Network use

  • AI model download (iPhone). When you download the on-device model, it's fetched once from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). As with any download, Hugging Face can see your device's IP address. No photos or personal data are sent.
  • iCloud Photos (iPhone). If your photos are stored in iCloud and you allow it in Settings, Apple's Photos framework downloads them from your own iCloud library so they can be analysed. This happens between your device and Apple under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Mac. The AI critique runs through Ollama on your own Mac (127.0.0.1); the app refuses non-local addresses. Ollama models are downloaded only when you install them yourself.

Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page. Questions: see Support.
