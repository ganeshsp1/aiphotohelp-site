AI Photo Help

Find the keepers in your shoot, privately.

AI Photo Help helps photographers cull a shoot. It finds soft, badly exposed and eyes-closed frames, groups bursts, critiques composition and moment with an AI model that runs on your device, and ranks a shortlist. Every recommendation shows its reasoning.

Private by design. Photos never leave your iPhone or Mac. There are no accounts, no analytics and no ads.
Non-destructive. Your photos are only read. The app never edits, moves or deletes them.
Learns your taste. Your Keep/Reject decisions, star ratings and quick "which is better?" answers teach it what you like.

Available for iPhone (iOS 18 or later; on-device AI critique needs an iPhone 15 Pro or newer) and for Mac (Apple Silicon).
